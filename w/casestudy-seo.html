SEO Rebuild

Multi-market site · SEO & Growth

The brief

A commercial site serving several markets had grown without a clear structure. Pages competed with each other, targeting differed by country and language, and technical issues limited how well search engines could crawl and understand it. The goal was a structure and content plan that worked for each market.

How we approached it

1
Audit
We audited crawling, indexing, structured data, and page performance to find what was holding the site back.
2
Restructure
We redesigned the site structure and URLs around markets and languages, and set up hreflang so each market sees the right version.
3
Re-plan the content
We mapped content to what customers in each market search for, and consolidated pages that were competing with one another.
4
Measure
We set up reporting so changes could be tracked against where the site started.
