The 6 metrics to track first

A starting guide for any business

If you only measure six things, start with these. Each one answers a single plain question about how the business is doing, and together they cover sales, cash, profit, stock, customers and operations. Pick the numbers that suit your business, then check them on the same day every week.

01 · SALES
Revenue
Are sales growing or declining?
02 · FINANCE
Cash flow
Can the business meet its obligations?
03 · PROFITABILITY
Gross margin
How much remains after direct costs?
04 · INVENTORY
Stock health
Is stock available without tying up too much cash?
05 · CUSTOMERS
Customer retention
Are customers returning and staying?
06 · OPERATIONS
Efficiency
How reliably and efficiently does work get done?
