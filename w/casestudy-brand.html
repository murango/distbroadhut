Brand + Site Launch

Early-stage startup · Branding & Development

The brief

An early-stage startup needed its brand and website ready to launch together, on a fixed timeline, with little room for rework.

How we approached it

1
Agree the brief and scope
We agreed the goals, audience, deliverables, and timeline up front, so there were no surprises on either side.
2
Build the identity
We designed the logo and wordmark, the colour and type system, and the tone of voice, and documented them in brand guidelines.
3
Design the site from the brand
We designed the website from the identity, so the brand and the site look and sound like the same company.
4
Build, test, launch
We built and tested the site, then launched and handed over, with post-launch support.
