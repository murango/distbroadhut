Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember preferences and understand usage patterns.

Essential Cookies

Required for the site to function. Include session management and security tokens. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

We do not currently run any analytics tools on this site. If that changes we will list them here and ask first. You can switch this on or off at any time in Cookie settings.

Preference Cookies

Remember your theme choice (light or dark mode) and your text size on this device. If you choose Essential only, we do not store them: the site follows your device setting and starts at normal text size each visit.

Managing Cookies

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect functionality. Visit allaboutcookies.org for more information.
