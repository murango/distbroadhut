Systems Architecture

Applications · Data · Processes · Teams

Applications usually get added one problem at a time: a tool for invoicing, another for stock, a spreadsheet to bridge the two. Each made sense alone. Together, they become a system nobody designed, like a rental block built one floor at a time with no plan for the plumbing.

For your business specifically, we document what you run today and define how your applications, data, processes, and teams should fit together as you grow. Then we decide what stays, what gets connected, and what needs replacing, and in what order.
