Reporting takes days, and nobody trusts the numbers

Common situation · Reporting

A situation we are often asked to help with. Many owners live with it for years because it feels like part of running a business. If it sounds like yours, here is what is usually behind it and how we fix it.

What you might be noticing

  • •Someone spends days gathering figures from different files.
  • •Two people give you two different totals.
  • •You decide on last month’s numbers, or on instinct.

What is usually going on underneath

Data lives in several places with no agreed source of truth, so each report has to be assembled by hand, and each assembly can come out differently.

How we fix it

1
Agree where each number lives
We decide which system is the source of truth for customers, stock, prices, and staff.
2
Connect the sources
Data flows from your systems into one place, without retyping.
3
Build the report once
The report updates itself, so the numbers are ready when you ask.
