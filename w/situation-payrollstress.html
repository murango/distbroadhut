Payroll is stressful every month

Common situation · Payroll & HR

A situation we are often asked to help with. Many owners live with it for years because it feels like part of running a business. If it sounds like yours, here is what is usually behind it and how we fix it.

What you might be noticing

  • •It takes days, and you hold your breath when it goes out.
  • •Staff questions about deductions land on one person.
  • •You’re not sure you could explain last year’s figures if asked.

What is usually going on underneath

Calculations and records are manual, so accuracy depends on one person and one spreadsheet, and there is no trail to answer questions.

How we fix it

1
Set the rules once
Salaries, allowances, and statutory deductions are configured, so every run follows the same rules.
2
Automate the run, review the result
The calculation runs itself, and your job becomes checking and approving.
3
Give staff their own records
Employees open their own payslips and history, and every change is recorded.
