Privacy Policy

Kenya Data Protection Act Compliant · Last updated: January 2025

1. Who We Are

Broadhut Studio is based in Nairobi, Kenya. We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. Data We Collect

Name, email, and project details via contact form; technical data (IP, browser, pages visited); and cookies as described in our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use It

To respond to enquiries, deliver services, send project communications, and improve our site. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis

Consent (contact form), contract performance (client services), and legitimate interests (analytics and security).

5. Retention

Contact enquiries: 2 years. Client project data: 7 years for legal/tax purposes. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

Access, rectify, erase, and port your data. Contact hello@broadhut.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at odpc.go.ke.
