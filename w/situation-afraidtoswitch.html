We’re afraid to switch systems in case we lose our data

Common situation · Data Migration

A situation we are often asked to help with. Many owners live with it for years because it feels like part of running a business. If it sounds like yours, here is what is usually behind it and how we fix it.

What you might be noticing

  • •You put off a change because the old system holds years of history.
  • •You’ve been told migration is risky, but nobody explained why.
  • •Records are duplicated, inconsistent, or half complete.

What is usually going on underneath

Without a mapped and tested migration, errors are copied across without anyone noticing. The fear is reasonable, and it is solved by process, not by hope.

How we fix it

1
Map every field
Each column in the old system is matched to its place in the new one before anything moves.
2
Clean during the move
Duplicates and inconsistencies are fixed on the way, with your team deciding the unclear ones.
3
Validate before go-live
We compare the result with your own records before anyone relies on it.
