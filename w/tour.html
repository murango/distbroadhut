What It's Like to Work Here

Nairobi, Kenya · Est. 2014

We're a small, progressive team that stays close to the problem end-to-end, whether that's a brand refresh or an ERP rollout, instead of everyone sitting in their own lane. Careers here grow by taking on more interesting problems, not by waiting for a title change.

🧩
Every Layer, One Team
From a CRM rollout to a brand refresh, the same team stays close to the problem end-to-end, so you're never boxed into a single layer of the stack.
🚀
Room to Grow
Small team, direct ownership. You'll ship work that reaches production fast, and take on more as you're ready for it, not on a fixed timeline.
🛠️
Tool-Agnostic by Design
We pick whatever's right for the problem, not one fixed toolkit. Our stack evolves as better tools show up, and so does what you learn using them.
🤝
Cohesive by Default
Design, development, strategy, and business systems sit close together, so decisions get made as a team instead of thrown over a wall.
