CRM Rollout

B2B services · Business Systems

The brief

A B2B services firm tracked enquiries and deals across WhatsApp, email, and personal spreadsheets. Follow-ups depended on individual memory, and management couldn’t see the pipeline without asking each salesperson. The goal was one shared record of every lead and deal.

How we approached it

1
Map how deals move
We mapped how an enquiry becomes a client today, including where leads arrive and where they stall.
2
Configure the pipeline
We set up lead capture and pipeline stages to match how the team already sells, instead of a generic template.
3
Automate the follow-up
We added reminders and next-step prompts, so follow-ups no longer depended on anyone’s memory.
4
Train and support
We trained the sales team on their own accounts, and stayed available after launch.
