We bought a system and nobody uses it

Common situation · Training & Adoption

A situation we are often asked to help with. Many owners live with it for years because it feels like part of running a business. If it sounds like yours, here is what is usually behind it and how we fix it.

What you might be noticing

  • •The old spreadsheet is still open on everyone’s screen.
  • •Staff say the new system is slower or confusing.
  • •Data in the new system is incomplete, so no one trusts it.

What is usually going on underneath

The system was installed but not fitted to how people work, and staff were not trained on their own tasks with their own data.

How we fix it

1
Find what is pulling people back
We look at where the old way is still easier, and why.
2
Fit the system to the workflow
We adjust screens, steps, and approvals to match how your team works.
3
Train by role, then follow up
We train each role on your own data, and check in after launch.
