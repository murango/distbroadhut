Accessibility Statement

WCAG 2.1 AA · Last updated: January 2025

Our Commitment

Broadhut Studio is committed to making our site accessible to everyone. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines and continuously improve our digital experiences.

Measures Taken

All interactive elements include ARIA labels and roles. The site is fully keyboard-navigable (Tab to move, Escape to close windows). Colour contrast meets AA standards in both themes. Fonts are scalable and the layout adapts to all screen sizes.

Known Limitations

Window drag-and-drop is a mouse-first experience. The menu bar and dock provide full keyboard access to all content without a mouse.

Feedback

If you experience accessibility barriers, contact hello@broadhut.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
