How Our Services Connect

One Architecture, Two Functions

Strategy sets direction and priorities. Enterprise Engineering is the operating architecture: how the business, its processes, data, and technology should work together. From there the work splits in two. Business Systems run the business: CRM, Sales, Payroll & HR, Inventory and Production, plus the custom modules, data migration and training that put them in place. Digital & Growth grows it: Design, Branding, Development, Motion and SEO. SEO is the link between the two, feeding qualified demand straight into CRM. Our Process is the thread through all of it: the same five stages apply whether we are mapping a workflow, implementing payroll, or launching a website.
