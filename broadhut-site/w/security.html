What stays with you

Security is shared. Some things only the business can do, and we help you put them in place and train your team on them.

🔑
Strong, unique passwords
Shared or reused passwords undo most other protections.
🚪
Removing access when someone leaves
Access should end the day a person’s role does.
🧭
Deciding who holds which role
We configure the roles, and you decide who needs them.
📣
Telling us early
If something looks wrong, tell us straight away so it can be checked.
