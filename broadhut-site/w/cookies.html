Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember preferences and understand usage patterns.

Essential Cookies

Required for the site to function. Include session management and security tokens. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Google Analytics 4 helps us understand how visitors interact with our site. All data is anonymised. You may opt out via your browser settings.

Preference Cookies

Remember your theme choice (light/dark mode) and other UI preferences across sessions.

Managing Cookies

Control cookies through browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect functionality. Visit allaboutcookies.org for more information.
