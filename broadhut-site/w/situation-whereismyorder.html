Customers keep asking where their order is

Common situation · Production & Operations

A situation we are often asked to help with. Many owners live with it for years because it feels like part of running a business. If it sounds like yours, here is what is usually behind it and how we fix it.

What you might be noticing

  • •Answering means walking to the floor or phoning a supervisor.
  • •Delays are discovered by the customer first.
  • •Instructions change as a job passes between shifts.

What is usually going on underneath

Jobs are not tracked as they move, so the status exists only in people’s heads.

How we fix it

1
Give every job a work order
Each job carries written instructions from start to finish.
2
Record each step as it happens
Job cards capture progress without interrupting the people doing the work.
3
Make status visible
Anyone who needs to answer a customer can see where the order is.
