Process & Workflow Design

Map How Work Moves

Most businesses run on workflows nobody has ever written down: how a quote gets approved, who tells the warehouse an order is ready, what happens when a client pays late. It works until the one person who holds it in their head is on leave.

For your business specifically, we sit with the people who do the work and map each process as it runs, from the first request to the final outcome, including the WhatsApp handoffs and workarounds. Then we mark where work stalls, repeats, or gets lost, and design the version that's worth building a system around.
