CRM

Customer Relationship Management

Right now, if a salesperson forgets to follow up, that lead is just gone, no record it ever existed. A CRM gives every lead a paper trail from first contact to signed deal, so nothing depends on one person's memory.

For your business specifically, this means every enquiry gets captured automatically, whether it comes from your website, an SEO landing page, or a walk-in, your pipeline mirrors how your team closes deals, and follow-ups fire on their own instead of living in someone's head or a notebook.
