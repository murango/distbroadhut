Inventory

Stock · Warehouses · Reorder Rules

"What's on the floor" and "what the system says" should be the same number, but for most businesses they're not, and that gap either costs you a sale or ties up cash in stock you didn't need. Inventory management closes that gap in real time.

For your business specifically, this means live stock counts across every warehouse or branch, batch and serial tracking if your product needs it, and reorder rules tuned to your lead times so you stop either running out or overstocking.
