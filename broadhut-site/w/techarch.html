Technical Architecture

Right Technology, Structured Around the Business

Technology choices tend to be made by whoever is in the room at the time: the tool a vendor demoed, the one a friend uses. Those choices are hard to undo once your data lives inside them.

For your business specifically, we assess the options against your workflows, budget, team, and growth plans, then recommend and structure a stack around the business rather than the other way round. The reasons for each choice are written down, so you know what you're committing to and why.
